Practice specific skills

The run is built out of these. On its own each one is calmer, and it is where you drill whatever the run keeps taking from you. There are three different things being trained here and it is worth knowing which is which.

Image hold — take a whole scene in and inspect it after it has gone.
Single-point focus — keep one object steady, and come back every time you slip.
Embodied scene — stand inside a short scene and make it real enough to answer from.
