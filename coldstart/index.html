Why these three and not a points system

Streaks and coins stop working in about four days, because an abstract number going up is not a reward an ADHD dopamine system reliably answers to — and once you break the streak, the app becomes a thing you're avoiding too. The bottleneck was never knowing or wanting. It's the gap between deciding and moving. So this only ever works on that gap.

Shrink the first move until it's stupid
Initiation is the whole fight; continuation usually takes care of itself once you're in. So the app refuses to let you plan. It makes you name one physical action under sixty seconds, then starts the clock immediately.
Behavioural activation; the "just five minutes" rule.
Make time physical
The core deficit is time blindness — time doesn't feel like it's passing. Digits don't fix that. A visible quantity draining across the screen does. That's why the bar is the biggest thing on the focus screen.
Barkley: ADHD as impaired self-regulation across time.
Catch thoughts without leaving
Sessions don't die from boredom, they die because something popped into your head and you chased it. An open loop keeps re-firing until it's written down somewhere. So there's a catch field on the focus screen and it never navigates away.
Zeigarnik effect; externalising working memory.
Give the worry an appointment
Rumination isn't solved by arguing with it. Scheduling it to a specific later time — and writing it down so your brain trusts it won't be lost — measurably cuts how much you worry the rest of the day. That's what parking a worry does here.
Borkovec: stimulus control for worry. Defusion wording is from ACT.
Two breaths in, one long breath out
The double-inhale sigh is the fastest voluntary way to bring arousal down in real time. It's forty seconds and it works on the body, not the argument.
Physiological sigh; cyclic sighing trials.
When something keeps sliding, wire it to a cue
"When X happens, I will Y" moves the trigger out of your executive function and into the environment. It's one of the best-evidenced behaviour-change moves there is, and almost no ADHD app uses it. Offered here after a task fails twice.
